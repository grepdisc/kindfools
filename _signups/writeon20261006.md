Register for WRITE ON! on Tuesday October 6th from 4:30pm to 5:45pm
at Buffalo State - Student Union as part of the Anne Frank Project's Social Justice
Festival.

Participation in any part of this *free* conference is welcome to anyone
without any need for advance registration. Buffalo State Students, however,
are encouraged to register in advance. Details on everything are **[available here](
https://www.annefrankproject.com/social-justice-festival)**. Please also
be sure to arrive early for this abbreviated WRITE ON workshop, which will feature insights
into how we create a space where people feel comfortable enough to do the work of
transformation during each WRITE ON.
